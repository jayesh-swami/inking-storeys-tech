Two street-facing volumes in plastered white and exposed brick trade places
across the elevation: brick recedes into a cantilevered balcony bay on the
upper floor, then reappears as a ground-floor accent beside the entry gate.
A vertical timber slat screen breaks up the stairwell facade, and a planted
parapet lines the rooftop terrace, softening the building's edge against the
sky.

The project is currently under construction.
