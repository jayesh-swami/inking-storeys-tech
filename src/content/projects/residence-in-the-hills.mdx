Set into a terraced hillside outside Jaipur, this residence was conceived as a
sequence of stone platforms rather than a single enclosed volume. Local
kota stone and lime plaster carry the material story from the retaining
walls into the interior floors, keeping the house cool through the
Rajasthan summer without leaning on mechanical systems.
